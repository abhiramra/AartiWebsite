Keerthana

Keerthana’s Equation – solving for a future in Maths

← Back to all stories

The school science fair was on when Keerthana was called to the Principal’s office and told that her mother had been killed.

Her father had been killed when she was four, and that case was never solved. She came to Aarti Village and put herself into her studies, because her mother had wanted her to become an officer. She cleared the first round of the Maths Olympiad and went on to the state level.

She is still at Aarti Village, and still working towards the officer’s job.
