Frequently Asked Questions

How can I help? ▼
You can donate, sponsor a child, or volunteer. See our donate page for the options, or contact us to talk through how you’d like to get involved.
Can I adopt a child from Aarti? ▼
No. Government regulations do not permit the adoption of children in our care.
How do children come to Aarti? ▼
Children come into our care in a few different ways:
  1. We find them during our visits to villages, or a village headman or concerned adult tells us about them.
  2. The district administration or the police refer them to us.
  3. Friends or relatives let us know about a child in need.
  4. We learn of them through the CHILDLINE 1098 helpline, awareness camps, or social investigation surveys.
What happens after you find a child in need? ▼
Our team carries out a social investigation to assess whether the child needs to be admitted to Aarti Home.
Can I sponsor a child, and how much does it cost? ▼
Yes, you can sponsor a child at Aarti. Our donate page lists the sponsorship options and their costs.
Can I volunteer at Aarti? ▼
Yes. Volunteers come from all over the world and usually stay between one and twelve months. See our volunteer page for details, and get in touch with any questions.
Do all the children attend Aarti School? ▼
Yes. Every child in our care studies at Aarti School.
How long does a child stay at Aarti? ▼
Until they can support themselves. There is no fixed date.
Is there a psychologist at Aarti? ▼
Yes, there is a psychologist on staff.
Do the girls marry when they grow up? ▼
If they want to, and when they want to. It is their decision.
Which children are supported under Family Based Care (FBC)? ▼
Family Based Care supports vulnerable children within their own family setting rather than in a residential home. You can find out who qualifies and how it works on our Care page.
How are children enrolled in the Family Based Care program? ▼
Before a child is enrolled, we carry out a social investigation to understand the family’s circumstances.
Can children living outside Kadapa join this program? ▼
At present the program is open to children whose parents live in Kadapa or nearby areas. Because it is difficult to bring children in from further away by school bus or other transport, those living with their parents outside Kadapa are instead given scholarships to continue their education.
How is a child’s progress monitored? ▼
We track each child’s progress through regular academic assessments and health checks.
How do the children get to school? ▼
They travel to school by school bus or by designated auto-rickshaws.
How do you make sure children attend school regularly? ▼
If a child is absent for more than two days, the Principal or class teacher calls the parents to check on the child’s welfare. Sometimes the parents already knew. Often they did not.
How are parents kept informed about their child’s progress? ▼
We hold regular parent-teacher meetings and one-to-one conversations with parents, so they always know how their child is doing.
Is there career guidance after the children finish school? ▼
Yes. The school runs career guidance sessions with students and their parents, discussing each student’s future based on their strengths and interests. We also support those who wish to continue academic studies or take up vocational training.
