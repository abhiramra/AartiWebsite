Frequently Asked Questions

How can I help? ▼
Please refer to our donations page for various donation options. For further information, please contact us.
Can I adopt a child? ▼
No, government rules do not permit adoption of the children at Aarti.
From where do the children come to Aarti? ▼
Children come to Aarti when:
  1. They are found during our visits to the villages, or we receive information from the village headman or a concerned adult.
  2. The district administration or the police inform us.
  3. Friends and relatives inform us.
  4. There is information through the CHILDLINE 1098, awareness camps, or social investigation surveys.
What is the follow-up process when you find a child in need? ▼
When we find a child in need, our team conducts a social investigation to assess the need for admission in Aarti Home.
Can I sponsor a child/children? How much does it cost? ▼
Yes, you can sponsor the children at Aarti. Please refer to our donation page for more details.
Can I volunteer at Aarti? ▼
Yes, we welcome volunteers from all over the world. Volunteers typically spend 1 to 12 months at Aarti. Please refer to our volunteer guidelines for more details, and get in touch with us for enquiries.
Do all the children attend Aarti School? ▼
Yes.
How many years does a child stay in Aarti? ▼
Till the child grows up to be a self-sufficient adult.
Do you have a psychologist at Aarti? ▼
Yes.
Do the girls get married when they grow up? ▼
Yes.
Who are the children that get care under Family Based Care (FBC)? ▼
Please refer to our Family Based Care information.
How are the children enrolled under this program? ▼
A social investigation is conducted to assess the family circumstances before enrolling the child in this program.
Can the children living outside Kadapa join this program? If not, what support is provided? ▼
This program is presently open to children whose parents live in Kadapa or nearby areas. Keeping in mind the difficulty in bringing children to school using school buses or other means, children living with their parent(s) outside Kadapa are provided with scholarships to continue their education.
How is the progress of a child monitored? ▼
The general progress of a child is monitored through regular academic assessments and health checks.
How are these children brought to school? ▼
These children are brought to school in the school buses or designated auto rickshaws.
How do you ensure that the child attends school regularly? ▼
Whenever a child is absent for more than 2 days, the Principal or the class teacher calls the parents to enquire about the child’s welfare. This process lets the parent and the teacher know if the child is being absent from school with or without the parent’s knowledge.
Is there a process to let parents know about the overall progress of the child? ▼
Regular parent-teacher meetings are conducted, and one-to-one interaction with the parents ensures that they are aware of their child’s progress.
Is there any career guidance for these children after they complete their schooling? ▼
The parents and the children attend a career guidance session organised by the school, and the students’ future careers are discussed with them keeping in mind their capabilities. Further support is extended to these students for continuing their academic studies or taking up vocational training.
