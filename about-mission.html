About Aarti

Mission, Vision & Core Values

What drives everything we do at Aarti For Girls.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every girl is celebrated, educated, and free to write her own story.

Our Mission

Our work is driven by four core pillars

Care

Providing a loving home for abandoned and orphaned girls, alongside the vital support families need to keep their daughters safe and thriving.

Educate

Unlocking every child's potential through quality formal education and dedicated community support.

Empower

Fostering independence for women through vocational training, sustainable livelihood opportunities, and strong support networks.

Advocate

Serving as a relentless voice for the vulnerable, campaigning against female feticide, and fighting for the inherent rights of every girl.

Our Promise

Whether a child lives in our care or learns in our classrooms, we promise them a childhood rooted in love and security. We heal insecurities, listen to their voices, and encourage them to dream without limits.

Our Guiding Values

Everything we do is built on deep compassion, absolute honesty, and a steadfast commitment to the women and children who need us most.
