Mana Bidda

2015 – 2017 · A three-year, EU-funded project

Mana Bidda set out to change how society sees women — with an ambitious end goal: a district that polices its own instances of gender discrimination. The project handled immediate cases as they arose, but its real focus was educating and shifting the mindsets of local leaders, so the fight for girls’ rights would continue long after Mana Bidda ended.

Seven pillars of action

Baseline assessment Village meetings Sensitisation sessions Leadership training Volunteer recruitment Advocacy efforts Psychosocial support
Sensitisation sessions were run in schools with young boys, where “role reversal” was a central theme — and were led by Aarti’s own girls. For most of the boys, they were an eye-opener.

What Mana Bidda achieved

  • ✓Kadapa’s girl-child ratio rose from 918 (2011) to 935 (2017) girls per 1,000 boys.
  • ✓1,215 village meetings reached 105,000 participants.
  • ✓119 abortions stopped.
  • ✓Girls’ school enrolment rose from 48.57% to 50.82%.
  • ✓Unregistered pregnancies fell from 551 in 2016 to just 8 in 2017.
  • ✓51 mandal-level and 70 panchayat-level support groups created to keep the work going.
  • ✓51 counsellors trained to support those facing gender discrimination beyond the project.
