Partner since 1992

Providing Possibilities Foundation (PPF)

PPF has been involved since 1992, the year Sandhya Puchalapalli founded Aarti Home.

In the early years PPF founder Roopa Reddy worked alongside Sandhya, raising money and finding people willing to back the idea. Once PPF was registered as a 501(c)(3), American donors had a straightforward way to give, and our income stopped being quite so unpredictable.

As Aarti expanded into Aarti Village, Aarti School and everything that followed, PPF stayed. Roopa now serves on the Aarti for Girls board, and the wider PPF community remains closely involved in opening up opportunities for girls and women.

Today it shows up at mealtimes. PPF funds a fifth of the food costs for every child across all of Aarti’s programs , which is a good deal of what makes three meals a day something we can count on rather than hope for.

Thirty-four years is a long time for anyone to keep turning up.
