Partner since 1992

Providing Possibilities Foundation (PPF)

PPF’s involvement with Aarti goes back to the very beginning — to 1992, the year Sandhya Puchalapalli founded Aarti Home.

In those first years, PPF founder Roopa Reddy worked at Sandhya’s side, raising money and gathering a circle of people who believed in what Aarti was trying to do. Once PPF was formally established as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, it became a dependable route for donors to reach the work — and the organisation grew steadier for it.

As Aarti expanded into Aarti Village, Aarti School and everything that followed, PPF stayed. Roopa now serves on the Aarti for Girls board, and the wider PPF community remains closely involved in opening up opportunities for girls and women.

Its most direct contribution today lands on the plate. PPF funds a fifth of the food costs for every child across all of Aarti’s programs — a large part of how we can promise each of them nutritious meals somewhere safe and properly looked after.

Across more than three decades, the partnership has come down to one shared conviction: that education, wellbeing and real opportunity are what change a girl’s prospects, and that the change is worth investing in for the long haul.
