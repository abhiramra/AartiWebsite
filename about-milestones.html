Milestones

Over three decades of growth — from six children to a way of life

1991

The First Step

Started in a humble rented space, opening our doors to provide a safe, loving haven for our first six abandoned children.

1996

Aarti Home is Born

Relocated to our first dedicated building, naming it Aarti Home, as our growing family of children rapidly outgrew the original premises.

1999

First Livelihood Programs

Launched our inaugural vocational and livelihood training programs, taking our first steps toward helping local women achieve financial independence and stability.

2000

Lalitha Women's Cooperative

Established the Lalitha Women's Cooperative, a dedicated tailoring initiative designed to empower women with sustainable livelihoods and a supportive community network.

2007

Global Recognition

Gained vital international recognition when our grassroots advocacy work was prominently featured in the acclaimed BBC documentary India's Missing Girls.

2008

Aarti English Medium School

Founded the Aarti English Medium School, bringing our vision of high-quality, empowering education to life for the children in our care.

2009

School Expansion

Expanded Aarti School into a brand-new, dedicated facility, proudly opening our classrooms to day students from the surrounding community.

2012

Childline Partnership

Appointed as the regional nodal organization for the 1098 Childline, significantly strengthening our ability to protect vulnerable youth across the district.

2014

Moving to Aarti Village

Moved our children to Aarti Village, a beautiful 2.5-acre green campus designed to provide a secure, nurturing, and holistic living environment.

2015

Mana Bidda Project

Launched the Mana Bidda Project, an intensive, community-driven three-year advocacy campaign fighting relentlessly against the practices of female feticide and infanticide.

2018

Abhaya Project

Initiated the Abhaya Project, a targeted three-year initiative dedicated to fiercely protecting, empowering, and supporting local women human rights defenders.

2020

COVID-19 Crisis Relief

Mobilized emergency COVID-19 relief for over 70,000 vulnerable families and supplied critical oxygen plants to drastically under-resourced local government hospitals.

2022

Aarti Women's Center

Opened the Aarti Women's Center, establishing a comprehensive, permanent safe space for community support, personal empowerment, and advanced vocational training.
