One Woman at a Time

A door-to-door campaign in Chennur & Chinta Komma Dinne mandals

Our team went home to home across two mandals — Chennur and Chinta Komma Dinne — sitting with pregnant women and their families to talk, honestly and one conversation at a time, about the value of a daughter and the rights every girl is owed.

Photos from the campaign — coming soon

What we talked about, door to door

  • •The value of daughters — spoken about directly with pregnant women and their families.
  • •Avoiding scans for sex determination of the child.
  • •Choosing not to abort a pregnancy on learning the baby is a girl.
  • •The law: foetal sex determination and sex-selective abortion are punishable offences under the PCPNDT Act — for the doctors who perform them as well as the parents and families involved.
  • •That domestic violence against women is a punishable crime.
  • •Enrolling every girl child in school.
  • •That child marriage is against the law.
