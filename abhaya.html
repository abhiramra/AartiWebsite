Abhaya (“Fearless”)

2018 – 2020 · An EU-funded project

Mana Bidda taught us that the hard part is not starting something, it is keeping it going once the funding ends. Abhaya put the women who had been doing this work into coalitions, so that none of them was standing alone in her own village, and set up the support they needed behind it: physical, psychological, social and legal.

The Abhaya App

We also built an Android app called Abhaya, so that finding out what the law says no longer depends on somebody knocking on your door to tell you. It is free.
