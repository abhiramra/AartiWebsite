Abhaya (“Fearless”)

2018 – 2020 · An EU-funded project

Abhaya grew out of what we learned from Mana Bidda: the momentum against gender discrimination had to be sustained. Its goal was to bring women together — building coalitions and networks — and to create a safe environment for continued activism across the mandals, with a physical, psychological, social, and legal support system for women human rights defenders.

The Abhaya App

As part of Abhaya, we built an Android app of the same name — putting knowledge of women’s rights a tap away for everyone. It’s free to download on any Android phone.
