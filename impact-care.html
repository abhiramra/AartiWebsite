Aarti Village

A sanctuary of love and belonging

For girls who have nowhere else to turn — many orphaned or escaping abuse — Aarti Village is a true home. On our green, sustainable campus, 145 girls live as sisters in small cottages, each guided by a house mother who builds a real family unit.

Here they find safety, healing, and the unconditional love to dream — growing into nurses, engineers, artists, and mothers who return to Aarti to celebrate every milestone of their lives.

Bhanu’s story

Bhanu was brought to Aarti Home at just two years old by her uncle, after her mother was killed. Within the care of the Aarti family, she found not only shelter but belonging and boundless opportunity.

She overcame early academic struggles with the unwavering support of her Aarti family, and — now with a growing passion for biology — she is working towards her dream of becoming a doctor.

Family Based Care

Nurturing dreams within a mother’s love

Seeing the dreams taking root at Aarti Village, we knew we had to reach children still living with their families but facing neglect, abuse, or the threat of early marriage and labour because of poverty.

Launched in 2018, Family Based Care supports education, nutrition, and daily necessities so children can stay in the love and security of their own homes. Our social workers visit regularly — offering both financial and emotional scaffolding — and a carefully designed five-step process works to break the cycle of hunger, abuse, and addiction.

Life at Aarti Village

Aarti is not an institution but a living home, where children can just be themselves.

🏡

Always Being There

Every girl who grows up and moves on stays part of the family. Aarti is their maternal home — a place they are always welcome to return to for support and celebration.

⚕️

Healthcare

Many of our committee members are local doctors, available at any time, and an in-house psychologist provides ongoing counselling for children who have been through trauma.

🧘

Daily Life

The girls live, eat, learn, and play in a home cared for around the clock. Mornings begin with yoga in the garden; evenings are for study and play; weekends for films, reading, and games.

💗

Bonding with the Children

We build strong bonds with every child, caring for each according to their needs — easing painful memories, building security, and welcoming new children into the family.
