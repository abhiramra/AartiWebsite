India Board of Directors

Ms. Sandhya Puchalapalli

President / Founder

Sandhya Puchalapalli founded Aarti For Girls. In 1992 she found a two-year-old girl abandoned on a street in Kadapa, left to starve after her father had killed her mother. She could not walk away from it. She and a handful of others set up the Vijay Foundation Trust, which began with six children. It now works with thousands of girls and women across Andhra Pradesh, and she still runs it.

United States

USA Board of Directors

Ms. Preeti Hari Nandanar

CEO

Preeti is the first CEO of Aarti for Girls Inc., the US organisation that supports Aarti India. Her grandmother worked on literacy among girls and women who had been left out of it, which is where Preeti’s interest started. She has supported Aarti India for over 30 years. Preeti brings over 15 years of experience in Financial Services, having worked in Investment Banking at JP Morgan and Citi. In 2017, she ventured into entrepreneurship, managing her own fashion label for six years. She took the Aarti role because she thinks educating girls is the part of the poverty problem that actually moves. An engineering graduate from IIT Chennai and an MBA from IIM Calcutta, Preeti resides in New York City with her family.

Ms. Deepthi Sigireddi

Senior Technologist

Deepthi is a senior technologist currently working at a Silicon Valley startup. Growing up as a girl in India, she saw the disparities in educational opportunities for girls and women born into economically disadvantaged families. It left her interested in what education actually does for girls and women in that position. Prior to joining the board of Aarti, Deepthi served as a mentor at her local Boys and Girls Club. She holds a Bachelor’s in Engineering from IIT Madras and a Master’s in Engineering from the University of Texas, Austin.

Ms. Bonnie Sue Zare

Professor

Bonnie is a Professor of Sociology and the Director of Women’s and Gender Studies at Virginia Tech. She has made yearly stays in India since 2002, focusing on Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. Her research centers on discourses of identity, feminism, and activism in contemporary India. Zare’s essays are published in Women’s Studies International Forum, Humanity and Society, and South Asian Review among others. Dr. Zare has brought seven groups of university students to Aarti to learn about on-the-ground change and build friendships.

Ms. Sridevi Joshi

Engineer

Sridevi originates from Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh and has been associated with Aarti Home since it was established in 1992. Inspired by Sandhya aunty’s dedication to helping the disadvantaged, particularly in improving the lives of girls and women, she aspires to be a part of this noble cause and make a meaningful contribution. Professionally, Sridevi is an Engineer and a proud alumna of JNTU Anantapur and BITS, Pilani. She holds the position of Senior Software Architect at Intel Corporation. Residing in Allentown, Pennsylvania, she shares her home with her son and daughter. In her leisure time, she enjoys traveling, painting, and gardening.

Ms. Subhashini Boyareddigari

Community Leader & Fundraiser

Subhashini is a dedicated person of service, who moved to the US in 1976 with her husband, a medical doctor. She is an experienced fundraiser and leader, having volunteered in hospitals and schools, and serving for 13 years as a board member of Hope Inc., an organization for battered women and children. Her mother tongue is Telugu, and she has served as the convener for several Telugu Association of North America and American Telugu Association conventions. Along with being a busy mother of two and grandmother of four, she has also been an Executive Member and President of her local temple.

Mr. Obul Kambham

Technologist & Entrepreneur

Obul Kambham, a seasoned technologist and entrepreneur with 40 years of experience, has contributed to major tech companies like Apple, Symantec, Cisco, and VMWare, as well as startups such as mPerpetuo and Automation Anywhere. He has successfully initiated three companies, leading to successful exits. His time at Apple emphasized the importance of creating human-centric and intuitive solutions. Currently, he mentors and supports multiple startups and is passionate about guiding young people based on his extensive experience.

Roopa Reddy

USA Board of Directors

Roopa is a philanthropist and advocate committed to improving the lives of children and underserved communities across the United States and India. She is Founder and CEO of Providing Possibilities Foundation, which funds child nutrition, education access, and care for people who are homeless. She has been associated with Aarti for Girls since our inception in 1992, with PPF partnering closely since 2009 to support fundraising, program development, and mental health initiatives, while continuing to strengthen Aarti’s work. PPF’s Banyan Tree Project, named for her sister Lali Reddy, funds mental health and LGBTQ+ support. She trained in biochemistry and molecular biology, and is a litigation attorney in California.

Shalini Puchalapalli

USA Board of Directors

Shalini turns around difficult businesses, and has run Sales, Finance, Supply Chain and HR to do it. She has built and run teams in Eastern and Western Europe, Asia and South America. She was honoured as a Young Global Leader (YGL) by the World Economic Forum in 2015 and recognized among Economic Times and Spencer Stuart’s 40 Under 40 Influential Leaders (2014) and India Inc’s Rising Women Leaders (2015). She has backed Aarti For Girls for over 30 years.
